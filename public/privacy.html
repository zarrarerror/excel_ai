Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025  ·  Shayntech

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use the Service:

  • Account data: email address and password (hashed)
  • Usage data: number of AI requests, tokens used, and feature interactions
  • Spreadsheet content: text you send to the AI assistant to process your requests. This is transmitted securely and not stored permanently.
  • Payment data: handled entirely by Paddle — we do not store card details

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Service
  • To authenticate your account and enforce usage limits
  • To send transactional emails (account, password reset)
  • To analyze aggregate usage patterns (no personal identification)

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase (hosted on AWS). We use industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Authentication tokens expire automatically.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • OpenAI: spreadsheet content you submit is sent to OpenAI's API to generate AI responses, subject to OpenAI's privacy policy
  • Paddle: payment processing and subscription management
  • Supabase: database and authentication hosting

5. Cookies

We use minimal session storage (no third-party tracking cookies) to keep you logged in. The add-in runs inside Microsoft Excel and does not place cookies on external sites.

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and data at any time by emailing us. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of material changes by email.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests: support@shayntech.com
